C.A.P.S.

— "Continuous Anomaly Protection System"

An autonomous road-safety platform that turns CCTV cameras into emergency responders. It watches the feed, decides whether an incident is real, and alerts the nearest authorities without a human in the loop.

  • Gemini analyses camera frames and rates each incident minor or major.
  • Major incidents alert the nearest hospital, police, fire and ambulance services at once, by WebSocket and Twilio voice call.
  • Each authority gets a 60 second countdown. A decline or timeout escalates to the next nearest.
  • Saves a snapshot and a 15 second clip per detection, tracks response times, and installs as a phone app.

EduChain

— "Academic token system on Avalanche"

Students earn EDU tokens for their grades and achievements. Each score is normalised against the whole college, so tokens get harder to earn as the college improves.

Tokens = 100 × (StudentScore / max(CPI, 1))
CPI = average of all StudentScores
  • The EduToken contract has owner-only minting and no approve or transferFrom, so tokens cannot be traded on a DEX.
  • Student registrations and the full mint history are stored on chain.
  • A FastAPI backend runs the scoring and calls the contract. An admin tab triggers distribution.

AlfaazVault

— "Words worth unlocking"

A marketplace where writers sell stories, poetry, shayari and essays for Sepolia ETH, and readers pay on chain to unlock and read them.

  • The full text never reaches the browser or public API until the backend confirms on chain that the reader has paid.
  • Gasless sign-in with a MetaMask signature (EIP-191), then a short-lived session cookie.
  • The backend checks each purchase receipt and matches the text hash against the hash emitted on chain.
  • Typography for English, Urdu (right to left) and Hindi.
